## Tumor Mutational Burden and the Immune Landscape of Glioma
The tumor mutation burden (TMB) Immune Landscape Glioma dataset contains a systematic analysis of TMB, expressed neoantigens, and the tumor immune microenvironment in adult diffuse gliomas, stratified by isocitrate dehydrogenase (IDH) mutation status, as published in Yu et al. Cancers 2021 ([PMID 34885201](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34885201/)). The cohort comprises 125 tumor samples from 48 patients profiled at the NCI Neuro-Oncology Branch under protocols [NCT02851706](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02851706), [NCT03718767](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03718767), and [NCT01109394](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT01109394). This adult glioma cohort provides a valuable comparative reference for pediatric glioma studies, offering insights into the relationships between TMB, neoantigen expression, antigen presentation, and the immune microenvironment that can be investigated in the context of pediatric disease.


### Clinical Data
Clinical data include patient demographics (Age at Diagnosis and Sex) and tumor histology (Cancer Type Detailed with IDH status and WHO grade). IDH Status (IDH-mutant vs IDH-wildtype) was extracted as a dedicated column from Cancer Type Detailed to enable stratification. Additional per sample clinical measures include Tumor Purity, Tumor Ploidy, TIL (Tumor Infiltrating Lymphocyte) Status and Fraction, per sample Tumor Mutation Burden (TMB, calculated as somatic mutations per megabase of covered exome), and a TMB Phenotype flag (Hypermutator/HMP if TMB > 10 mutations per megabase, Non-Hypermutator/NHMP otherwise). Immune repertoire attributes include T-cell receptor (TCR) clonotype count and diversity (across TRA, TRB, TRG, and TRD chains), and B-cell receptor (BCR) clonotype count and diversity (across IGH, IGK, and IGL chains).


### Mutation Data
Somatic mutations were called on tumor–normal paired whole exome sequencing (WES) using the NCI ClinOmics Bioinformatic Pipeline v3.2, per Yu et al. 2021, Section 5.4. MuTect was used for somatic SNV calling and Strelka for small indel calling.

High confidence somatic mutation filters applied:
* Tumor total coverage ≥ 20×
* Variant allele frequency (VAF) ≥ 10%

Filtering reduced the raw MAF from 65,112 rows across 125 samples to 34,432 high confidence rows across 65 tumor samples (matching the paper's reported cohort).


### Copy Number Data
Copy number data were provided as segment level log2 values. Data processing and subsequent display in cBioPortal includes:

##### Parsing Segment Level Data
Segment level log2 copy ratio values from source pipeline output were parsed and reformatted into the required cBioPortal tabular structure. Each segment was mapped to genomic coordinates along with its corresponding log2 value.

##### Gene Level Mapping
Segment data were intersected with UCSC hg19 refGene coordinates and assigned gene level copy ratio values via overlap weighted mean.

##### Discrete CNV Calls (GISTIC-like)
Gene level log2 values were binned into GISTIC-like discrete calls using:
* Amplification (+2): log2 > 2.0
* Gain (+1): log2 > 0.3
* Neutral (0): -0.3 to 0.3
* Shallow deletion (-1): -1.0 to -0.3
* Deep deletion (-2): log2 < -1.0


### Structural Variant Data
RNA-Seq fusion calls were generated by combining tophatFusion, FusionCatcher, STAR-fusion, and Arriba. Fusions detected by multiple tools were combined into a single call and only those supported by at least 2 callers were retained. This reduced 11,385 raw fusion calls to 553 high confidence fusions across 57 samples.
