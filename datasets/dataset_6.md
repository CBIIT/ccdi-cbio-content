## Genomic Sequencing of Pediatric Cancer Cases (CMRI/KUCC)
This study provides paired tumor-normal genomic sequencing data from ~100 children with cancer, including solid tumors and leukemias, generated by the Children's Mercy Research Institute (CMRI) and University of Kansas Cancer Center (KUCC). The dataset includes mutation calls from whole genome sequencing (WGS) and whole exome sequencing (WXS), gene expression data from bulk RNA sequencing, somatic structural variant calls, and associated clinical data for participants and samples. Samples were also labeled to indicate whether variant calls were available from WXS, WGS, or both. The underlying raw sequencing, derived alteration and clinical data can be viewed in the [CCDI Hub Explore](https://ccdi.cancer.gov/explore?dbgap_accession=phs002529) under study accession phs002529, with access to controlled-access data requiring authorization through [dbGaP](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/projects/gap/cgi-bin/study.cgi?study_id=phs002529).


### Clinical Data
Clinical data (demographic, diagnosis, anatomic site and overall survival) were extracted from data submitted to the CCDI. The clinical data were then formatted for ingestion and visualization in the CCDI cBioPortal.


### Mutation Data
The [Genome Nexus Annotation Pipeline](https://github.com/genome-nexus/genome-nexus-annotation-pipeline) was used to annotate raw, PASS-filtered somatic variant calls from VCF files with the minimum annotations required for cBioPortal ingestion and to convert VCFs to MAFs. Variants were annotated with MSKCC curated list of isoforms for transcripts. Post-processing included concatenating the individual MAFs together for loading into CCDI cBioPortal and performing quality checks to identify faulty/mis-formatted annotations. Mutations were then labeled with their source type, i.e. “Variant Type” field as either “WES” or “WGS” for downstream filtering by users. Additionally, tumor variant allele fraction (VAF) data was parsed from source VCF files.

Since approximately one-third of source somatic VCF files were generated from tumor-only calling, additional post-hoc germline filtering was performed on somatic mutation calls. This filtering included:
*	Removing variants called on the Y chromosome
*	Removing variants with VAF >0.95
*	Comparing variants with hits in gnomAD and removing those with observed allele frequencies > 0.001

Upon data loading, cBioPortal [automatically filters out](https://docs.cbioportal.org/file-formats/#variant-classification-filter) variants classified as Silent, Intron, IGR, 3’UTR, 5’UTR, 3’Flank, and 5’Flank, except for promoter mutations of the TERT gene.


### Structural Variant Data
Six samples had somatic structural variant (SV) data provided in VCF files generated by the [Severus](https://github.com/KolmogorovLab/Severus) somatic structural variant calling tool. SV data were parsed from the VCF files, and their breakpoints were mapped to gene locations using GENCODE 48. The resulting data were then formatted into cBioPortal SV format for ingestion. Of the 3,904 SV records, 1,457 did not have one or both breakpoints located outside annotated gene regions and were therefore excluded loading into cBioPortal.


### RNA-Seq Expression Data
Expression data files containing the transcripts per million (TPM) normalized counts for profiled transcripts were concatenated together across samples. Ensembl transcript IDs were then mapped to HUGO gene symbols using the Ensembl v.116 gene transfer format (GTF) file available from the [Ensembl FTP](http://ftp.ensembl.org/pub/current/gtf/homo_sapiens/) site. For genes represented by multiple transcripts, transcript-level TPM values were summed within each sample to obtain a gene-level TPM value. The resulting data were converted to matrix format for ingestion into the CCDI cBioPortal.

To generate z-score normalized expression data, the gene-level expression matrix was processed using the cBioPortal [NormalizeExpressionLevels_allsampleref.py](https://github.com/cBioPortal/datahub-study-curation-tools/tree/master/zscores/zscores_relative_allsamples) script. Z-scores were calculated using all samples as the reference population, independent of sample diploid status.
