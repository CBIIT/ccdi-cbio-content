## NCI-COG Pediatric MATCH Precision Medicine Clinical Trial
PedMATCH’s underlying raw sequencing data can be searched through the [CCDI Hub Explore](https://ccdi.cancer.gov/explore?dbgap_accession=phs002883). Controlled-access data is available via dbGaP authorization under [phs002883](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/projects/gap/cgi-bin/study.cgi?study_id=phs002883).

### Clinical Data
Clinical data, including diagnosis, diagnosis age, survival and treatments, were sourced from Children’s Oncology Group (COG)-provided clinical data files. 


### Mutation, Copy Number, and Structural Data
Single nucleotide variants (SNV), Copy Number Alteration (CNA), and Fusion data were sourced from variant-customized Oncomine panel VCFs which were generated by exome sequencing at PedMATCH-designated CLIA-compliant labs. When duplicate participant VCFs were encountered, the most recently generated VCF file was used. 1,168 participants with both tissue and blood VCF files were formatted and loaded into CCDI cBioPortal. The [Genome Nexus Annotation Pipeline](http://github.com/genome-nexus/genome-nexus-annotation-pipeline) was used to prepare the MAF files with annotation. 

##### Mutation Data
Variants from tumor VCFs were compared with normal VCFs and filtered where: 
* Tumor Dimerization Partner (DP) >= 20 and Tumor Allele Frequency (AF) >= 0.05 (high read count of alternate allele) 
* Normal DP >= 15 and Normal AF <= 0.02 (low to no read count of normal allele) 
* Tumor Variant passes quality checks (FILTER = PASS) 
* Tumor Variant is not homozygous to reference allele (i.e. Genotype != 0/0) 
* Tumor variant genotype != normal genotype 

A resulting 382 participants had at least one SNV mutation parsed.  

##### Copy Number Data
Gene-level CNVs were reported in Oncomine VCFs with observed Copy Number (not log2 transformed) in clinical VCFs. Since Oncomine was only validated on copy number alterations with Amplifications, only CNVs reported with Amplification annotation were parsed. Any amplified genes from a Tumor Sample also amplified in the corresponding Normal Sample were removed. Resulting parsed amplifications were then either log2 transformed or binned into discrete values (see Discrete CNV Calls (GISTIC-like) under MCI Copy Number Data processing) for display in cBioPortal. A total of 254 participants had at least one gene with copy number amplification.  

##### Structural Variants (Fusions) Data
Normal and Tumor fusions were compared based on 5’ and 3’ genes and exon numbers involved and has a PASS filter (as opposed to NOCALL or FAIL). Overlapping fusions with the same genes and exons in both Tumor and Normal VCFs of a given patient were removed. A total of 183 participants had at least one fusion event.
